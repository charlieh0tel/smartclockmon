smartclockmon for Windows

Console programs, built and tested by CI on Windows but untried on a
receiver there, and unsupported: the supported releases are the Debian
packages beside this.  There is no installer and no service.  How the
programs differ on Windows is in docs/running.md, under Windows:
https://github.com/charlieh0tel/smartclockmon/blob/main/docs/running.md
