# 58503A command tree

Every command path in `third_party/58503a-3633.bin`, read out of the
parser's own tables the way `z3801-tree.md` was read out of the
Z3801A's: a node is a record whose long at +0 names its keyword, whose
long at +4 names its child list, and whose query handler sits at +18
and setter at +8 (`firmware.md`, "Note on the disassembly").  The
image is revision 3633; the bench receiver runs 3704-C.

`?` marks a node with a query handler and `(set)` one with a setter.
A path being here means the parser of revision 3633 knows it, not that
any receiver answers it, and nothing here was sent to a receiver for
this document.

## Against the command table

116 of the 130 `hp58503` entries in `commands.toml` appear here with
their spelling, including 38 whose only evidence was a manual page.
The fourteen that do not:

- `:SYNChronization:HOLDover:DURation:THReshold`, its setter and
  `:EXCeeded?`: here the node is
  `:ROSCillator:HOLDover:DURation:MEASurement:THReshold`, as in the
  Z3801A image; the bench 3704 answers the short spelling.
- The nine `:SYSTem:COMMunicate:SERial1:...` settings: here `SERIAL1`
  is a leaf with one handler and the settings sit under
  `:SYSTem:COMMunicate:SER:RECeive:` -- `BAUD`, `BITS`, `PACE`,
  `PARity:TYPE`, `SBITs` -- and `:SER:FDUPlex`, `:SER:PRESet`.  The
  bench 3704 answers the `SERial1:BAUD?` spelling.
- `:DIAGnostic:ERASe` belongs to the INSTALL language, as on the
  Z3801A.
- `:DIAGnostic:ROSCillator:EFControl:DATA?` has no node: the `EFControl`
  branch here holds only `ABSolute` and `RELative`.  The bench 3704
  answers it.
- `:DIAGnostic:SLOG?` is a node with children and no handler of its
  own; the bench 3704 answers it.

So three commands the bench receiver answers are not in this
revision's tree, which is the first concrete difference found between
3633 and 3704.

## Against the Z3801A tree

508 paths here against 513 in `z3801-tree.md`; 340 are common.
Only here: the developer commands `GARY`, `DOUGlas`, `KENneth` and the
four-letter factory tokens, `:DIAGnostic:TMODe`, `:DIAGnostic:REFerence:
EXTernal|GPSystem:QUEStionable:HYSTeresis`, `:GPSystem:...:ADELay:FEET`,

`:OUTPut:PINn` delays and frequencies, `:LED:TMHValid`, the
