# 58503A command tree

What the command trees of the 58503A images say, against the command
table.  The paths themselves are `scpi/58503a-3633.txt` (914) and
`scpi/58503a-3704.txt` (931), as `smartclock-cli dump-scpi` reads them
out of the parser's own tables (`scpi/README.md`, which also compares
them with the other images).  The bench receiver runs 3704-C.

A path there means the parser knows it, not that any receiver answers
it.  Nothing was sent to a receiver for this document.

## Against the command table

119 of the 130 `hp58503` entries in `commands.toml` are in the 3633
tree with their spelling, and 120 in the 3704's.  Those that are not:

- `:SYNChronization:HOLDover:DURation:THReshold`, its setter and
  `:EXCeeded?`: the node is
  `:ROSCillator:HOLDover:DURation:MEASurement:THReshold`, as in the
  Z3801A image; the bench 3704 answers the short spelling.
- The six `:SYSTem:COMMunicate:SERial1:...` entries: `SERIAL1` shares
  `SER`'s child list, whose settings sit under `:RECeive:` -- `BAUD`,
  `BITS`, `PACE`, `PARity:TYPE`, `SBITs` -- with `:FDUPlex` and
  `:PRESet` beside it.  The bench 3704 answers the `SERial1:BAUD?`
  spelling.
- `:DIAGnostic:ERASe` belongs to the INSTALL language, as on the
  Z3801A.
- `:DIAGnostic:ROSCillator:EFControl:DATA?`, in 3633 only: its
  `EFControl` holds only `ABSolute` and `RELative`, and 3704 adds
  `DATA` and `MODE`.  The bench 3704 answers it.

`:DIAGnostic:SLOG?` is a node with children and no handler of its
own in both; the bench 3704 answers it.
